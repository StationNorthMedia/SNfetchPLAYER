SNfetchPLAYER is an open-source media broadcast and audio engine designed for Android smartphones, tablets, and Android TV. Built for music enthusiasts and media curators, SNfetchPLAYER turns your Android device into a complete TV and Radio broadcast station.

KEY FEATURES:

• SN-TV Mode (16:9 60 FPS Video Engine):
Powered by AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer draped in custom slanted lower-third Bauchbinden displaying real-time track metadata and station branding. Features full HUD OSD touch and Android TV D-Pad remote control.

• SN-RADIO Mode (60 FPS Audio Engine):
Equipped with a real-time 60 FPS FFT frequency spectrum visualizer synchronized with deep bass and R&B audio streams. Displays dynamic Station North quotes and glowing ambient highlight colors.

• Curator Studio (Command Center):
Pre-configured with a 100-hit main mix. Supports pasting YouTube playlist links or IDs to extract and append video streams instantly, community playlist sharing (JSON / URLs), and full catalog curation.

• SN-Lexicon (4,438+ Entity Encyclopedia):
A comprehensive music encyclopedia covering Urban Culture, US Hip-Hop, and R&B legends, complete with discographies, tracklists, and detailed artist biographies.

• Optimized for Smartphones, Tablets & Android TV:
Full D-Pad remote navigation support alongside intuitive touchscreen controls. Provides customized layouts for portrait phones and widescreen 10-inch tablets or TV setups.

• Lightweight & Remote Asset Cloud Delivery:
Lazy background synchronization of jingles and TV station IDs to keep the initial application download size minimal.

• Open Source & Privacy Focused:
No advertising, no tracker SDKs, no user telemetry, and full user sovereignty over your broadcast signals.
