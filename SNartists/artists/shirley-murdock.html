Shirley Murdock

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Shirley Murdock is an American R&B singer-songwriter. She is best known for her guest appearance alongside Charlie Wilson on Zapp and Roger's 1986 single "Computer Love", as well as her 1986 single, "As We Lay". The latter peaked at number 23 on the Billboard Hot 100 and spawned from her self-titled debut album (1986), released by Elektra Records.
