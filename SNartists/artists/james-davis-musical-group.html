James Davis

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
JAMESDAVIS is an American R&B trio based in Los Angeles. It is composed of three siblings: fraternal twins Jess and Rey Reynolds and their brother Auston Reynolds. The trio has released several EPs, including most recently, Masterpeace, in May 2019. JAMESDAVIS is currently signed to Motown.
