Kobe

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Brian Honeycutt, better known by his stage name Kobe, is an American R&B singer and songwriter from Chicago, Illinois. He is perhaps best known for his guest performances on songs by American rapper Eminem—including 2010's "Talkin' 2 Myself" and "Cinderella Man", and 2014's "Die Alone"—as well as songs produced by DJ Khalil.
