Beans Bowles

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Thomas Harold "Beans" Bowles was an American jazz and session musician, primarily known as a baritone saxophonist and flutist with Motown Records' house band, the Funk Brothers. He played the flute solo on the studio version of Stevie Wonder's "Fingertips", and arranged "Fingertips Part II." Beyond performing, Bowles served as a tour manager for the Motortown Revue, musical director for acts like Smokey Robinson & the Miracles, and an influential mentor in Detroit's music scene.
