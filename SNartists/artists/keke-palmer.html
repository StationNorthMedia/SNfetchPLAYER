Keke Palmer

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Lauren Keyana "Keke" Palmer is an American actress, singer, and television host. She has received numerous accolades, including two Primetime Emmy Awards and nominations for an Actor Award and three Daytime Emmy Awards. Time magazine included her on its list of most influential people in the world in 2019.
