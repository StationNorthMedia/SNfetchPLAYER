Barbara Tucker

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Barbara Tucker, is an American house and soul singer, songwriter and choreographer born in Brooklyn, New York. Tucker had six No. 1 hits on the U.S. Hot Dance Club Songs chart in the 1990s and into the 2000s, and several hits in the UK.
