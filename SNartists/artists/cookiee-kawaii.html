Cookiee Kawaii

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Vanice Brittany Palmer, known professionally as Cookiee Kawaii, is an American rapper and singer. She is best known for her song "Vibe ", which became popular on TikTok in 2020. After the song went viral, she signed to Empire, and released her debut studio album, Vanice, in 2021.
