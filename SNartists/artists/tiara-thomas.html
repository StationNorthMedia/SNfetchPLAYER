Tiara Thomas

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Tiara Nicole Thomas is an American R&B singer. She is best known for her guest appearance on Wale's 2013 single "Bad", which peaked within the top 40 of the Billboard Hot 100 and received triple platinum certification by the RIAA. That same year, Thomas signed with Rico Love's Division1 record label, an imprint of Interscope Records.
