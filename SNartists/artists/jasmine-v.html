Jasmine V

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jasmine Marie Villegas, known professionally as Jasmine V, is an American singer. She was signed to Damon Dash's label Dame Dash Music Group at the age of 12 and RCA Records at the age of 16 before signing to Interscope Records and Ester Dean's Eye Know a Secret (E.K.A.S.) Records.
