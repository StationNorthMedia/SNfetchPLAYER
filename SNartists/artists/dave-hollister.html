Dave Hollister

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
David Le Chaine Hollister is an American singer from Chicago, Illinois who found fame in 1994 as one quarter of the R&B group Blackstreet, before going on to have a solo career. Hollister is best known for his 2000 gold-certified album Chicago '85... The Movie, which included the singles "One Woman Man" and "Take Care of Home". He was also featured on 2Pac's hit singles "Brenda's Got a Baby" and "Keep Ya Head Up". He was a member of the band United Tenors.
