Millie Jackson

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Mildred Virginia Jackson is an American R&B and soul recording artist. Beginning her career in the early 1960s, three of Jackson's albums were certified gold by the RIAA for over 500,000 copies sold. Jackson's songs often include long spoken sections, sometimes humorous, sometimes sexually explicit. According to the cataloguing site WhoSampled.com, her music has been sampled in over 300 songs.
