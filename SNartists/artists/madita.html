Madita

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Edita Malovčić, known by her stage name, Madita, is an Austrian singer and actress of Bosnian descent. Her father is Bosnian folk singer Kemal Malovčić. Madita's music ranges from synth-pop to R&B to jazz.
