Lou Pride

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Lou Pride was an American blues and soul singer and songwriter. Some sources state his year of birth was 1950. He is best known for his compositions "Long Arm of the Blues" and "Love From a Stone". Pride had a cult following among British Northern soul aficionados.
