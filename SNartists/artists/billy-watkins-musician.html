Billy Watkins

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Robert Bartha L. "Billy" Watkins was a gospel and song singer who later became a Christian minister. He was also founder of the gospel singing group, The Zion Travelers. He recorded for the Arwin, Challenge, Chess, Era, Imperial, Kent and Victor labels.
