Susaye Greene

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Susaye Greene is an American singer and songwriter. She was the last official member to join the Motown girl group The Supremes, remaining in the group during its final year of existence from 1976 to 1977. She is a successful songwriter as well, having written hit records for Michael Jackson, Deniece Williams, and others.
