Rachid

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Rasheed A. Bell, better known by his stage name Rachid, is an American singer-songwriter and music producer. He is the son of founding Kool & the Gang member Ronald Bell. He achieved a top 40 hit on the Dance Club Songs chart with his debut single, "Pride" (1998).
