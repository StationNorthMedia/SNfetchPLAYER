Wayne Cochran

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Talvin Wayne Cochran was an American singer, known for his outlandish outfits and platinum blond pompadour hairstyle. He was sometimes referred to as The White Knight of Soul. Cochran is best known today for writing the song "Last Kiss", which he performed with the C.C. Riders.
