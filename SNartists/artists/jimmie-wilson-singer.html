Jimmie Wilson

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jimmie Wilson is an American singer and musical theatre actor. He represented San Marino in the Eurovision Song Contest 2017, with the song "Spirit of the Night" in a duet with Sammarinese singer Valentina Monetta but failed to qualify for the final.
