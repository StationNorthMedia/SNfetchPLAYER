N3wyrkla

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Nyla Naalin Nasir, known professionally as N3wyrkla, is an American singer-songwriter and rapper. Nasir rose to prominence with her guest performance alongside ASAP Rocky on Brent Faiyaz's 2023 song "Outside All Night" for his debut mixtape Larger than Life. The song peaked at number 1 on the Billboard Bubbling Under Hot 100, and received a gold certification by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA). Earlier that year, she released her debut extended play (EP), Selfishly Entitled (2023).
