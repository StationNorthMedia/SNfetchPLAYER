Eric Gable

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Eric Gable is an American soul/R&B singer. From 1988 to 1994, he released three studio albums, being signed to Orpheus Records/Epic Records, and had a number-one R&B hit "Remember ". Another single, "Process of Elimination", peaked at number sixty-three in the UK Singles Chart in March 1994, his only UK chart entry.
