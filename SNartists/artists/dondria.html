Dondria

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Dondria Nicole Fields, known mononymously as Dondria, is an American R&B singer. She is best known for her 2009 single "You're the One", which peaked at number six on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 chart. The song preceded her debut studio album Dondria vs. Phatfffat (2010), which was released by So Do Def and Geffen Records, and peaked at number 51 on the Billboard 200.
