H.E.R.

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Gabriella Sarmiento Wilson, known professionally as H.E.R. is an American R&B singer-songwriter. She has received an Academy Award, a Children's and Family Emmy Award, and five Grammy Awards, as well as nominations for a Golden Globe Award, three American Music Awards, and four Billboard Music Awards.
