Johnny Daye

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Johnny Daye was an American soul music singer from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, who released six singles between 1965, when he signed to Danny Sims and Johnny Nash's Jomada record label, and 1968 recording two tracks on Stax Records. In 2007 he came out of retirement to sing on two tracks on Robert Peckman's first solo CD, Stirrin' Up Bees.
