Daniel Merriweather

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Daniel Paul Merriweather is an Australian R&B singer-songwriter and record producer. Merriweather's debut solo album, Love & War, was released in June 2009. It entered the UK Albums Chart at number two. It was preceded by two singles, "Change" and "Red", which both made the top 10 on the related singles chart. Merriweather has won two ARIA Music Awards, Best Urban Release in 2005 for "She's Got Me" and Best Male Artist in 2009 for Love & War.
