Nikka Costa

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Domenica "Nikka" Costa is an American singer whose music combines elements of pop, soul, and blues. She began her career as a child singer in the early 1980s. She is the daughter of music producer Don Costa.
