Jobete Music

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jobete Music Company, Inc. is the music publishing division of Motown Records originally established by Motown founder Berry Gordy, and his wife Raynoma Liles Gordy in Detroit, Michigan. As the publishing arm of Motown, Jobete was responsible for the publishing, administration, and licensing (copyright) of the songwriting output created by Motown’s staff of songwriters, composers, and artists. Over the following decades, Jobete grew into one of the most valuable popular music publishing catalogues in the world, controlling the rights to most of Motown’s best-known songs.
