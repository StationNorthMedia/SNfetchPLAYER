Justin Nozuka

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Justin Tokimitsu Nozuka is an American singer, songwriter, and actor of Japanese and Canadian descent. His debut album, Holly, was released in 2007. He has since published five more studio albums: You I Wind Land and Sea (2010), Ulysees (2014), Run to Waters (2018), Daydreams & Endless Nights (2023), and Chlorine (2024).
