Nigel Hall

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Nigel David Hall is an American vocalist and keyboardist. He is a member of Lettuce and also fronts his solo project, The Nigel Hall Band. He was born and raised in Washington, D.C., and lives in New Orleans, Louisiana.
