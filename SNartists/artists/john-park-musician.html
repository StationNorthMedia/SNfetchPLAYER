John Park

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
John Andrew Park better known by the stage name John Park, is an American singer-songwriter. He was a semi-finalist on the ninth season of American Idol, placing eighteenth. He is the runner-up of Superstar K 2, a Korean singing contest held by M.net. He is currently signed to Music Farm Entertainment.
