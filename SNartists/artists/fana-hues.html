Fana Hues

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Fana Subira Hughes, known professionally as Fana Hues, is an American R&B singer, songwriter and multi-instrumentalist from Pasadena, California. Hues gained prominence in 2021 after being featured on the Tyler, the Creator song "Sweet / I Thought You Wanted to Dance".
