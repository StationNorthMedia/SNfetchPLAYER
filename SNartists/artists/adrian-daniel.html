Adrian Daniel

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Adrian Nelson-Daniels, better known by his stage name Adrian Daniel, is an American singer, songwriter, and producer from Brooklyn, New York of African descent. Daniel has released one EP and three albums: Fool (2014), Disillusions (2016), FLAWD (2018), and Nightwolf (2020). Daniel's music is described by BET and Okayplayer as Neo-Soul.
