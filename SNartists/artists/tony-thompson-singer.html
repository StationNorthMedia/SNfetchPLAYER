Tony Thompson

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Tony Ulysses Thompson was an American singer–songwriter. Thompson was best known as the lead vocalist of the American R&B quintet Hi-Five, which had hit singles such as "I Like the Way " and "I Can't Wait Another Minute". After the group disbanded in 1994, Thompson found solo success the following year with his debut album Sexsational in 1995.
