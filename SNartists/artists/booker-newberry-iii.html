Booker Newberry III

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Booker Newberry III was an American singer and keyboardist, who was a member of the mid-1970s soul groups Sweet Thunder, and Impact, before pursuing a solo career. He is probably best remembered for his 1983 hit "Love Town".
