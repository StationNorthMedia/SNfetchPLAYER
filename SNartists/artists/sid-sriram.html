Sid Sriram

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Sidharth Sriram known as Sid Sriram is an Indian Carnatic musician, music producer, playback singer. He is an R&B songwriter and has been working in the Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi, Marathi and English music industry. He regularly collaborates with his sister Pallavi Sriram, a Bharatanatyam dancer, and music director.
