Scherrie Payne

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Scherrie Ann Payne is an American singer. Payne is best known as a member and the co-lead singer of the R&B/Soul vocal group the Supremes from 1973 until 1977. Payne is the younger sister of singer Freda Payne. She continues to perform, both as a solo act and as a part of the "Former Ladies of the Supremes" (FLOS). She previously performed as the lead singer of The Glass House.
