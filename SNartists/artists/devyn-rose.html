Devyn Rose

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Tanya Dormevil, known professionally as Devyn Rose, formerly known as Tanya T6, is an American singer-songwriter from New York City. She has released a number of singles, of which "Falling 4 U" reached No. 1 on the Billboard Biz Hot Singles Sales Chart. The latter was included on the EP Stellar, released in July 2015. In August 2018 she released the album Thir$ty.
