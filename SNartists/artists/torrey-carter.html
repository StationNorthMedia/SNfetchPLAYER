Torrey Carter

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Torrey Carter is an American R&B singer. He gained popularity after signing to Missy Elliott's The Goldmind Inc. as the first male artist under the label. There, he began recording for his debut album, The Life I Live, which was led by the single "Take That". However, due to the lack of mainstream success with the lead single, the album was shelved and he departed from Goldmind.
