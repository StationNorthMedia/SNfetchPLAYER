Tasha Thomas

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Tasha Thomas was an American singer and actress, known for her role as Aunt Em in the original Broadway production of The Wiz. Thomas also had a hit single, "Shoot Me ", from her 1979 album, Midnight Rendezvous. Her birthplace is often cited as "Jeutyn, Alaska", though no such town exists.
