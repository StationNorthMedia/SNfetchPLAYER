Bryant Barnes

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Bryant Barnes, is an American singer-songwriter and musician. He originally gained virality on TikTok through piano covers, but later signed with Mercury Records and began to put out R&B music. He released his first extended play, Vanity in 2024. It contained the hit song "I'd Rather Pretend", which was later made into a remix featuring d4vd. His debut studio album, Solace, was released on October 17, 2025.
