Frankie Valli

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Francesco Stephen Castelluccio, better known by his stage name Frankie Valli, is an American singer and occasional actor, best known as the lead vocalist of the Four Seasons. He is known for his unusually powerful countertenor voice.
