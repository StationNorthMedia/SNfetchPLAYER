Nodesha

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Nodesha is an American R&B singer, who was signed to Arista Records when she was only sixteen years old. She released her debut album, Nodesha, in 2003. Jimmy Jam, Terry Lewis, and L.A. Reid were executive producers on the album. Nodesha co-wrote all of her songs. Nodesha released three singles: "Get It While It's Hot", "That's Crazy" and "Miss Perfect" featuring Five member Abs.
