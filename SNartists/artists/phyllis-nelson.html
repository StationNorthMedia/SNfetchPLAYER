Phyllis Nelson

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Phyllis Nelson was an American singer, most famous for her 1985 song "Move Closer", which topped the UK Singles Chart. Her son is the singer and record producer Marc Nelson, who was a member of Boyz II Men.
