Ann Nesby

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Ann Nesby is an American singer, songwriter, actress, and producer known for her work in R&B, gospel, and dance music. She rose to fame as the lead vocalist of the Grammy Award-winning group Sounds of Blackness before launching a solo career that has earned her multiple Grammy nominations and critical acclaim.
