Lester Williams

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Lester B. Williams was an American Texas blues and electric blues guitarist, singer and songwriter. He is best known for his song "Winter Time Blues" and "I Can't Lose with the Stuff I Use". His main influence was T-Bone Walker.
