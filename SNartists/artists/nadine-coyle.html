Nadine Coyle

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Nadine Elizabeth Louise Coyle is an Irish singer-songwriter, producer and model. In 2002, she was selected as a member of Girls Aloud, a pop girl group created through ITV's reality competition show Popstars: The Rivals. The group went on to receive large success, achieving a string of 20 consecutive UK top ten singles, two UK number one albums, five consecutive platinum selling studio albums, and receiving nominations for five BRIT Awards, winning Best Single in 2009 for "The Promise".
