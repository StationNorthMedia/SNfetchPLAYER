Jozzy

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jocelyn Adriene Donald, better known by her stage name Jozzy, is an American songwriter and singer. She is best known for co-writing the Billy Ray Cyrus remix of Lil Nas X's 2019 single "Old Town Road", which peaked atop the Billboard Hot 100 for a record-breaking 19 weeks. Her other credits include the top ten singles "Lemonade" by Internet Money and "Mr. Right Now" by 21 Savage and Metro Boomin, as well as "Virgo's Groove" by Beyoncé, "Low" by SZA, and "You" by Jacquees, among others.
