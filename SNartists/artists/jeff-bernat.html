Jeff Bernat

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jeff Bernat is a Filipino-American singer, songwriter and record producer. He debuted in 2012 with the album, The Gentleman Approach, and developed a significant fan base in South Korea after the album track, "Call You Mine," was featured on the 2014 Korean drama, You Are My Destiny.
