Slim

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Marvin Scandrick, known professionally as Slim, is an American contemporary R&B singer best known as the lead singer/frontman for the group 112. His 2008 debut album Love's Crazy peaked at No. 32 on the Billboard 200. It included the hit singles "So Fly", which reached No. 49 on the Billboard Hot 100, and "Good Lovin'", which reached No. 39 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart.
