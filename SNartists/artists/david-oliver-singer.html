David Oliver

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
David Oliver was an American soul singer best known for the quiet storm radio hit, "Ms" as well as his composition "Friends & Strangers", which was covered by Ronnie Laws in 1977. He also recorded the first version of the Cecil Womack song "Love TKO", releasing it as an album track on Here's To You in 1980.
