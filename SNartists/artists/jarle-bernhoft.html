Jarle Bernhoft

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jarle Bernhoft, known professionally as Bernhoft, is a Norwegian singer, musician and songwriter. His best known songs are "Streetlights", "Shout", "Choices", "C'mon Talk" and "Stay with Me". He has also been in a number of bands, notably Explicit Lyrics and Span.
