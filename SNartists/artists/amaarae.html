Amaarae

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Ama Serwah Genfi, known professionally as Amaarae, is a Ghanaian-American singer and songwriter. She is known for her fusion of pop, R&B, Afrobeats, and alté, as well as her fluid representation of gender and sexuality. After collaborating with local artists and putting out a few non-album singles, she released her debut EP, Passionfruit Summers, in 2017.
