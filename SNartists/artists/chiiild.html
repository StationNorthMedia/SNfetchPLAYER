Chiiild

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Chiiild is a Canadian experimental soul band, fronted by Yonatan "xSDTRK" Ayal signed to Avant Garden Records and 4th & Broadway. The Montréal-based band formed in 2017 and released their debut EP, Synthetic Soul, in 2020.
