Johnny Powers

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
John Leon Joseph Pavlik, known professionally as Johnny Powers, was an American guitar player, singer, writer and producer specializing in rockabilly. Powers was best known for his 1957 recording on the now-defunct Fox Records label entitled "Long Blond Hair".
