Jimmy Sweeney

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
James Sweeney Jr. was an American singer, songwriter, and self-taught guitarist. He was a member of the Nashville African-American music scene, and a veteran of World War II. As a pop singer, he was known professionally as Jimmy Sweeney a.k.a. Jimmy Bell. Jimmy was born, raised, and died in Nashville, Tennessee.
