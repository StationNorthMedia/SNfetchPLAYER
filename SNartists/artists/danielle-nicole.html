Danielle Nicole

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Danielle Nicole is an American blues/soul musician born in Kansas City, Missouri, United States. Her self-titled solo debut EP was released March 10, 2015, on Concord Records. The self-titled EP features Grammy Award-winning producer and guitarist Anders Osborne, Galactic's co-founding drummer Stanton Moore, and keyboardist Mike Sedovic. On February 25, 2015, American Blues Scene premiered the track "Didn't Do You No Good" off the EP.
