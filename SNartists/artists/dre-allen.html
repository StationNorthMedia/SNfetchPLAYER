Dre Allen

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Andre "Dre" Allen is an American actor, singer, songwriter, music video director, and producer. Born in California's Bay Area, Dre Allen is also the founding member of Los Angeles-based 1990s pop and R&B group IV Xample, whose debut album For Example sold over 500,000 copies worldwide due to the success of their debut single "I'd Rather Be Alone".
