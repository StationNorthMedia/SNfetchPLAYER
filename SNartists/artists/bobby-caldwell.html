Bobby Caldwell

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Robert Hunter Caldwell was an American singer, songwriter, and musician. He released several albums spanning R&B, soul, jazz, and adult contemporary, and was known for his soulful and versatile vocals. He released the hit single and his signature song "What You Won't Do for Love" from his double platinum debut self-titled album in 1978.
