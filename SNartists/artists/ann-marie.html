Ann Marie

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Joann Marie Slater, known professionally as Ann Marie, is an American singer and rapper signed to Interscope Records. She is best known for her single "Secret", which has garnered over 100 million views on YouTube and peaked at number 22 on the Billboard Hot R&B Songs chart. Her debut EP Pretty Psycho was released on July 10, 2019.
