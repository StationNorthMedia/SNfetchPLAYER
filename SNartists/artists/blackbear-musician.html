Matthew Tyler Musto, known professionally as Blackbear, is an American musician, singer, songwriter and record producer. He has released six studio albums, six EPs, one mixtape and two collaborative albums. Musto is also a member of the alternative hip-hop duo Mansionz with singer Mike Posner.
