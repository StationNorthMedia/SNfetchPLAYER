B Angie B

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Angela Roxanna Boyd, known by the stage name B Angie B, is an American R&B singer and dancer. Originally a backup vocalist for MC Hammer, she rose to prominence with the release of her debut self-titled album in April 1991 on Bust It Records. The album spawned two US Top 5 R&B singles: "I Don't Want to Lose Your Love" and "So Much Love".
