Sailorr

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Kayla Le known professionally as Sailorr, is an American singer and songwriter. She is known for her major-label debut single, "Pookie's Requiem", which peaked at number 8 on the Bubbling Under Hot 100. Her debut mixtape From Florida's Finest was released on May 9, 2025. Grammy named her one of the "10 rising artists making R&B more diverse and experimental than ever."
