Cissy Houston

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Emily "Cissy" Houston was an American soul and gospel singer and choir director. Nicknamed The Duchess of Gospel, she is best known as the mother of American singer and actress Whitney Houston and the aunt of American singers Dionne Warwick and Dee Dee Warwick.
