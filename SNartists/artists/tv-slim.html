T.V. Slim

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Oscar W. Wills known professionally as T.V. Slim, was an American blues and R&B singer, guitarist and songwriter. His best selling work was "Flat Foot Sam", which helped propel his recording career through to the date of his death.
