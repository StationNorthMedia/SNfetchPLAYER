DRAM

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Shelley Marshaun Massenburg-Smith, known professionally as DRAM, is an American rapper, singer, and record producer. He is best known for his 2016 Diamond certified single, "Broccoli", which peaked at number five on the US Billboard Hot 100. The song preceded his debut studio album Big Baby DRAM (2016), which was released in October of that year by Atlantic Records.
