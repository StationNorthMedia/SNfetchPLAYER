Big George Brock

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
George Brock, billed as Big George Brock, was an American blues musician. A native of Mississippi, he moved to Missouri in the 1950s and operated a series of nightclubs. He played alongside Muddy Waters, Howlin' Wolf, Jimmy Reed, and Albert King. Brock starred in the 2006 film Hard Times, a documentary about his life.
