Matt B

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Matthew David Benson, better known by his stage name Matt B, is an American singer, songwriter, musician, and record producer. In 2022, Benson's, Gimme Love, was nominated for a Grammy Award for Best Global Music Performance and charted at Number 36 on Billboard's US Afrobeats Songs chart. In 2025, he won a Grammy Award for "Best Global Music Album" for ALKEBULAN II.
