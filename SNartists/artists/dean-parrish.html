Dean Parrish

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Dean Parrish was an American singer from New York City, best known for the song "I'm on My Way", which became noted for being the penultimate record that was played at the last Northern soul all-nighter at the Wigan Casino. In the 2000s, in collaboration with Lord Large, he had a minor hit with "Left Right & Centre".
