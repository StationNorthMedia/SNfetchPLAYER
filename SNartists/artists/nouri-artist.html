NOURI

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Vivian Nouri is a New Zealand recording artist of Kurdish origin. On 6 November 2018, NOURI released her debut single "Where Do We Go From Here". The song debuted at number one on the New Zealand Hot 20 Singles Chart. On 8 March 2019, NOURI released her second single "Favourite Goodbye".
