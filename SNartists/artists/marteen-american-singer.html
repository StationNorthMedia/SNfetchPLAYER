Marteen

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Marteen Estevez, known mononymously as Marteen, is an American singer and songwriter. He has opened for acts such as Kehlani, Fifth Harmony, Dua Lipa and NCT 127. Record producer J. R. Rotem discovered Marteen and has produced many of his songs.
