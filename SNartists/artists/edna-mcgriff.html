Edna McGriff

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Edna McGriff was an American rhythm and blues singer and pianist. She recorded mainly in the 1950s, and secured one hit single on the US Billboard R&B chart with "Heavenly Father" in 1952. The record sold over 500,000 copies.
