Aliff Aziz

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Mohammad Aliff bin Aziz is a Singaporean singer and actor based in Malaysia. He is managed under Sony Music BMG Malaysia. In July 2007, he was crowned the winner of a reality TV singing competition, Anugerah, making him the youngest winner of the show/competition. In September of that same year, Aziz released his first self-titled album with single "Cinta Arjuna'" with 300,000 copies sold around Southeast Asia. His first single, "Sayang Sayang" received the most number of ringtone downloads (1 million) across Southeast Asia. Song was inspired by Sham Faisal who was told to have a very strong influence on the singer.
