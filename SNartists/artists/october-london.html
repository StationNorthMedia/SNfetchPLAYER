October London

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jared Samuel Erskine, known professionally as October London, is an American singer-songwriter. He signed to Death Row Records by Snoop Dogg in 2022 and released his studio album, The Rebirth of Marvin, in 2023. Its single, "Back to Your Place", topped the Billboard Adult R&B Songs chart and was nominated for three Soul Train Music Awards.
