Arthur Maia

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Arthur Maia was a Brazilian composer and musician. Maia played with musicians such as Djavan, Gilberto Gil, Marisa Monte, Lulu Santos and Ney Matogrosso. His compositions usually present a fusion of jazz, funk, swing music and reggae.
