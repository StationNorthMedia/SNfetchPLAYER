Bridget Kelly

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Bridget Kelly is an American singer and songwriter from New York City. Kelly, an R&B recording artist, has written songs for Kelly Clarkson. In 2008, she secured a recording contract with American rapper and hip-hop mogul Jay-Z's record label Roc Nation.
