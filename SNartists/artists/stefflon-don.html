Stefflon Don

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Stephanie Victoria Allen, known professionally as Stefflon Don, is an English rapper. She rose to fame with her 2017 single "Hurtin' Me", which peaked at number seven on the UK Singles Chart. In 2016, Allen released her debut mixtape Real Ting, followed by her second, Secure (2018).
