Jody Watley

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jody Vanessa Watley is an American singer, songwriter, and music producer. Watley first found musical success in 1977 when she became a part of the original lineup of the R&B group Shalamar. The group recorded the hits "The Second Time Around", "A Night to Remember" and "Dead Giveaway". After recording several albums with the group, Watley left the group in 1983.
