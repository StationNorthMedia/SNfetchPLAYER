Jalen Ngonda

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jalen N'Gonda is an American musician based in England. He came to prominence with the release of his debut studio album Come Around and Love Me (2023), and is known for his Motown-inspired soul music. N'Gonda is primarily a singer, but also plays various instruments during live performances.
