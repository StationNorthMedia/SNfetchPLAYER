Peppermint

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Peppermint, or Miss Peppermint, is an American actress, singer, songwriter, television personality, drag queen, and LGBTQ activist based in New York City. She competed on the ninth season of RuPaul's Drag Race in 2017, finishing as the runner-up. Peppermint made her debut in The Go-Go's-inspired musical Head Over Heels in 2018, becoming Broadway's first out trans woman to originate a lead role.
