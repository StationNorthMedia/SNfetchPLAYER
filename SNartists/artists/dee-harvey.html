Dee Harvey

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Demetrius La Vell "Dee" Harvey was an American R&B singer. He was at one time signed to Motown and recorded his first album, Just as I Am with Motown in 1991.
