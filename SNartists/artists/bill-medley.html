Bill Medley

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
William Thomas Medley is an American singer best known as one of the Righteous Brothers. He is noted for his bass-baritone voice, exemplified in songs such as "You've Lost That Lovin' Feelin'". Medley produced a number of the duo's songs, including "Unchained Melody" and "(You're My) Soul and Inspiration".
