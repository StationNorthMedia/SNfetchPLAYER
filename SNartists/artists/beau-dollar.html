Beau Dollar

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
William Hargis Bowman Jr., better known by his stage name Beau Dollar, was an American soul vocalist and drummer for King Records. He performed on many studio albums for various artists under contract with King, including James Brown. His most prominent work was performed as "Beau Dollar & The Dapps" and "Beau Dollar & The Coins".
