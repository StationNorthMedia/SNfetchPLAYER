Kristi Martel

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Kristi Martel, is a singer, songwriter, performance artist and yogini based in Rhode Island. She has recorded much of her music, and has toured extensively across the United States and Canada, giving concerts in many cities. She also teaches classes in a variety of yoga methods.
