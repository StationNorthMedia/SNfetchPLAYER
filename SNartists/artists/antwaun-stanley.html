Antwaun Stanley

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Antwaun Stanley is an American singer and songwriter based in Ann Arbor, Michigan. He is the recurring vocalist of the funk band Vulfpeck. His solo album, titled I Can Do Anything, charted on the Billboard Gospel charts in 2006. His most recent extended play Ascension, with musician Tyler Duncan, was released in 2021.
