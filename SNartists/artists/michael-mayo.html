Michael Mayo

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Michael Mayo is an American singer and composer. He has performed and toured with Kneebody, Jacob Collier, Christian Sands, Josh Groban, Nate Smith and Herbie Hancock. He released his debut studio album Bones in 2021, followed by his sophomore album Fly in 2024.
