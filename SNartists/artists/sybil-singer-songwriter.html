Sybil

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Sybil Ilona Lynch, known mononymously as Sybil, is an American R&B and pop singer-songwriter. Sybil gained notable success in her career with songs during the mid-1980s into the mid-1990s. She is the cousin of En Vogue singer Maxine Jones.
