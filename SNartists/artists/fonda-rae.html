Fonda Rae

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Fonda Rae is an American R&B singer best known for her club hits like "Over Like a Fat Rat" and "Touch Me". She has also worked with artists such as Don Armando, Kid Creole and the Coconuts, Taka Boom, The Fat Boys and Debbie Harry.
