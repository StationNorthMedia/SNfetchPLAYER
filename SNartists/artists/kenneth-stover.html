Kenneth Stover

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Kenneth C. Stover was an American Motown songwriter, producer, and singer. He contributed background vocals on Marvin Gaye's 1971 album What's Going On and wrote the first draft of Gaye's 1973 single "Let's Get It On".
