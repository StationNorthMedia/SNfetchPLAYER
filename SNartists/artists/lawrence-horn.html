Lawrence Horn

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Lawrence Thomas "L.T." Horn was an American musician, record producer and chief recording engineer for Motown Records in Detroit and Los Angeles. He later served three consecutive life sentences for hiring a hit man to murder his ex-wife, Mildred Horn, their disabled son, Trevor, and nurse Janice Saunders.
