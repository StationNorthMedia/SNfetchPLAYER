Ron Holden

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Rolan Webster "Ron" Holden was an American pop and rhythm and blues singer from Seattle, Washington, United States. He appeared on The Lloyd Thaxton Show, Mike Douglas Show, American Bandstand and The Dick Clark Show. He performed at the Apollo Theater with artists Jackie Wilson, the Crests and Redd Foxx. Most notable were USO tour-stops with Elvis Presley, Pat Boone and Connie Francis.
