Angela Winbush

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Angela Lisa Winbush is an American R&B/soul singer-songwriter, musician and record producer who rose to fame first in the 1980s R&B duo René & Angela, also scoring hits as a solo artist. Winbush has sold over 10 million albums and singles worldwide.
