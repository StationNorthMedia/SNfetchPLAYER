Ashley Roberts

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Ashley Allyn Roberts is a British-American television presenter, singer, dancer and actress. She is a member of the R&B and pop group the Pussycat Dolls. With two albums and over 55 million records sold worldwide, the Pussycat Dolls are one of the world's best-selling female groups of all time. Roberts also had a brief career as a solo singer with the album Butterfly Effect (2014).
