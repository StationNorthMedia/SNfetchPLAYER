Karencici

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Karen Lam, better known by her stage name Karencici, is an American singer-songwriter. She is known for her Mandopop, R&B and hip-hop influenced music which often blends Mandarin and English lyrics. She is also a member of PanThePack, a Chinese hip-hop group formed by Jackson Wang in 2021, alongside Wang, J.Sheon, and Ice.
