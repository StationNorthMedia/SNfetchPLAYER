Kym Sims

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Kym Sims is an American singer whose biggest success came outside her home market, most notably the United Kingdom and Scandinavia. Sims began her career as an advertising jingle singer before breaking through into the dance music market. Following a battle with alcohol addiction, Sims returned to the Billboard Dance charts, reaching number 6 with "Turn It Up" in 2017.
