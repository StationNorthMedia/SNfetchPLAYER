Nivea

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Nivea B. Hamilton, known mononymously as Nivea, is an American singer. She has released three studio albums: her self-titled debut (2002), followed up by Complicated (2005), Animalistic (2006), and an independently released extended play, Nivea: Undercover (2011). In 2019, she released her album Mirrors.
