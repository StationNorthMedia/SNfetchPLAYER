Selah Sue

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Sanne Greet A. Putseys, known professionally as Selah Sue, is a Belgian musician and songwriter. Her debut album, Selah Sue (2011), was a major success, selling over 720,000 copies in Europe, 320,000 in France alone.
