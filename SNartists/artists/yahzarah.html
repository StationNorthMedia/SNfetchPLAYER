Yahzarah

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Dana Nicole Amma Williams, known professionally as YahZarah, is a Ghanaian-American singer and music producer. She is known for radio hits "Wishing" and "Why Dontcha Call Me No More". She has released four albums, with two reaching the Billboard Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart.
