Jordan Knight

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jordan Nathaniel Marcel Knight is an American-Canadian singer and songwriter. He is the lead vocalist of the boy band New Kids on the Block (NKOTB), which rose to fame in the 1980s and 1990s, using a falsetto style of singing influenced by The Stylistics. After New Kids on the Block split in 1994, he launched a solo career.
