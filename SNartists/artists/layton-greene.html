Layton Greene

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Layton Greene is an American R&B singer from East St. Louis, Illinois. She is best known for her 2019 single "Leave Em Alone", which peaked at number 60 on the Billboard Hot 100 and received platinum certification by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA). Previously in January of that year, she signed with Georgia-based record label Quality Control Music as its first female R&B artist. Her debut extended play (EP), Tell Ya Story, was released on September 9, 2019.
