Royce Jones

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Royce Jones is an American musician best known for his work as a backing vocalist with the bands Steely Dan and Ambrosia. In the studio, Jones contributed vocals to Ambrosia's David Pack's Anywhere You Go, one song on Steely Dan's Countdown to Ecstasy, Bruzer's Round 1 and Stephan Cohn's self-titled release.
