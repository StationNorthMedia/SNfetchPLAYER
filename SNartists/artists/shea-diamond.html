Shea Diamond

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
ShaGasyia "Shea" Diamond is an American singer, songwriter, and transgender rights activist. Her music is chiefly soul and R&B, and includes elements of blues, rock, hip-hop and folk. Her debut extended play Seen It All was released on June 29, 2018.
