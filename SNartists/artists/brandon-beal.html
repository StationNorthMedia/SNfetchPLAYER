Brandon Beal

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Brandon O'Bryant Beal is an American singer, songwriter, and record producer based in Denmark. He is best known for his 2014 single Twerk It Like Miley, which topped the Danish Singles Chart and went viral internationally through the app Dubsmash. Beal also reached number 1 in Denmark with "Golden" in 2016, and was featured on Christopher's chart-topping single CPH Girls.
