Lee Oskar

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Lee Oskar is a Danish harmonica player, notable for his contributions to the sound of the rock-funk fusion group War, which was formed by Howard E. Scott and Harold Brown, his solo work, and as a harmonica manufacturer. He continues to play with 3 other original War band members, Harold Brown and Howard Scott, under the name Lowrider Band.
