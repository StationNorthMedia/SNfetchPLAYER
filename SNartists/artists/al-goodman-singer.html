Al Goodman

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Willie Albert Goodman was an American singer and writer who performed as part of the musical trio Ray, Goodman & Brown, a group that was earlier called The Moments and was known for their songs "Love on a Two-Way Street", "Sexy Mama" and "Look at Me " as The Moments and later, "Special Lady" after changing their name to Ray, Goodman and Brown.
