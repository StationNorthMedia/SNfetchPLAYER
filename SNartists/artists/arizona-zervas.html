Arizona Zervas

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Arizona S. Zervas is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter. He is best known for his 2019 single "Roxanne", which peaked at number four on the Billboard Hot 100 after being featured in Spotify playlists. The success of the song led him to tour nationwide after its release, and in November of that year, he signed with Columbia Records. "Roxanne" also topped the charts in New Zealand, and reached the top ten in the UK, Australia, and several other countries. His debut studio album, Wild West (2023), was released independently and entered US Apple Music charts.
