Don Toliver

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Caleb Zackery "Don" Toliver is an American rapper, singer and songwriter. In 2026, he landed his first number-one album on the Billboard 200 with Octane, which included his first No. 1 singles on the Billboard Hot Rap Songs chart, “E85” and “Body”. The album spent 13 weeks at number-one on the Billboard Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart, and all 18 of the album’s tracks hit the Billboard Hot 100.
