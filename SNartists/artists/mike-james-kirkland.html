Mike James Kirkland

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Mike James Kirkland is an American R&B singer. He began his recording career in 1965, as leader of Mike & The Censations, a quartet which included his brother Robert, their sister and her husband. He was also half of the duo Bo and Ruth on Claridge Records who achieved a U.K. Top 20 single with their release of "You're Gonna Get Next To Me" in the summer of 1977.
