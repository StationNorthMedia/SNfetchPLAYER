Serayah

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Serayah Ranee McNeill, known mononymously as Serayah, is an American actress, model, and singer. She is known for her roles as Tiana Brown on the Fox musical drama series Empire (2015–2020), and as Rebecca "Rbel" Belle in the BET+ musical drama series, Kingdom Business (2022–2023).
