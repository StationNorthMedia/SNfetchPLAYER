Janice Robinson

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Janice Robinson is an American singer and songwriter. She is known for being a member of the 1990s Eurodance group Livin' Joy with whom she released the global dance hit "Dreamer" in 1994. She departed from the group following its release and embarked on a solo career, releasing her debut album The Color Within Me in 1999.
