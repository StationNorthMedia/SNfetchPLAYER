Ricky Bell

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Ricardo Bell is an American singer and rapper, best known as one of the founding members of R&B/pop group New Edition, and the lead singer of Bell Biv DeVoe. As a solo artist, Bell released the album Ricardo Campana in 2000.
