Eddie Levert

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Edward Willis Levert is an American singer best known as the lead vocalist of the O'Jays. He is the father of sons Edward Jr, Gerald (1966–2006) and Sean Levert (1968–2008) and daughter Ryan Levert (2002–2024).
