Molly Grace

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Molly Grace Zeytoonian (born December 6, 2001), is a queer independent American musician and singer-songwriter based out of Nashville. Grace has referenced artists such as Lizzo, Chappell Roan, Lawrence, Reneé Rapp, Remi Wolf, Taylor Swift and Freddie Mercury as influences in her music that blends pop, funk and disco.
