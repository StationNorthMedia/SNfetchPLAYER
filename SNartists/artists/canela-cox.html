Canela Cox

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Canela Cox, known mononynously as Canela, is an American R&B singer and songwriter. She is best known in the mixtape circuit for her DJ Clue-remixed song, "Sponsor", and for co-writing Anastacia's worldwide top 20 hit "Why'd You Lie to Me". She once recorded an album in 2001, however it was shelved after her label folded.
