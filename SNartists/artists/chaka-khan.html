Chaka Khan

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Yvette Marie Stevens, known professionally as Chaka Khan, is an American singer and songwriter. Known as the "Queen of Funk", her career has spanned more than five decades beginning in the early 1970s as the lead vocalist of the funk band Rufus. With the band she recorded the hits "Tell Me Something Good", "Sweet Thing", "Do You Love What You Feel", and the platinum-certified "Ain't Nobody".
