Delara

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Amanda Delara Nikman, known by her stage name Delara, is a Norwegian and Iranian singer. Starting her career in 2016 with debut single, "Paper Paper", she has released four EPs and one studio album, Shahrazad (2023). In 2024, her song "Siste dans", from the film The Snow Sister, reached number-one in Norway.
