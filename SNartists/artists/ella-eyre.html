Ella Eyre

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Ella Mary McMahon, known professionally as Ella Eyre, is an English singer and songwriter. She rose to prominence following her collaboration with Rudimental on their UK number-one single "Waiting All Night" (2013), which won the Brit Award for British Single of the Year. She also featured on DJ Fresh's single "Gravity" (2015). She was the runner-up for the 2014 Brit Critics’ Choice Award and is known for her work in dance-pop and soul-influenced music.
