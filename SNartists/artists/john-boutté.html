John Boutté

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
John Boutté is an American jazz singer based in New Orleans, Louisiana, who has been active since the mid-1990s. He is known for a diverse music style that goes beyond jazz to R&B, gospel, Latin, and blues. He is the younger brother of the jazz and gospel singer Lillian Boutté, and a relative of cartoonist George Herriman.
