Alphonso Williams

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Alphonso Williams was an American soul singer, active in Germany. He won the fourteenth season of Deutschland sucht den Superstar. During his participation in DSDS, he came up with the stage name Mr. Bling Bling, which he then continued to use.
