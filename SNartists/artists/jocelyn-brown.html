Jocelyn Brown

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Jocelyn Lorette Brown, sometimes credited as Jocelyn Shaw, is an American R&B and dance singer. She has a Billboard Hot 100 chart entry solely in her name. Brown sang on 23 hit singles on the UK singles chart, eight of which have reached the top 20.
