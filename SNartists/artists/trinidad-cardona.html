Trinidad Cardona

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Trinidad Cardona is an American singer, songwriter and social media personality. He first went viral for his song "Jennifer" released in 2017. His single, "Dinero", peaked at 46 on the Billboard Global 200 after becoming viral on TikTok. In 2022, he released the song "Love Me Back". His 2022 song, "Hayya Hayya ", was included on the 2022 FIFA World Cup Soundtrack.
