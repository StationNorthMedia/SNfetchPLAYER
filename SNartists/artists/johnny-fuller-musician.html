Johnny Fuller

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Johnny Fuller was an American West Coast and electric blues singer and guitarist. Fuller showed musical diversity, performing in several musical genres including rhythm and blues, gospel and rock and roll. His distinctive singing and guitar playing appeared on a number of 1950s San Francisco Bay Area recordings, although he ceased performing regularly by the late 1970s.
