Debbie Sledge

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Debra Edwina Sledge, known professionally as Debbie Sledge, is an American singer–songwriter. She rose to fame in the 1970s as a member of Sister Sledge, one of the highest-selling female groups of the 1970s. In 2021, she formed a spinoff called Sister Sledge featuring Sledgendary.
