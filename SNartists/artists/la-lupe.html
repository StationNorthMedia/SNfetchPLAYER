La Lupe

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Lupe Victoria Yolí Raimond, better known as La Lupe, was a Cuban singer of boleros, guarachas and Latin soul known for her energetic, sometimes controversial performances. Following the release of her first album in 1961, La Lupe moved from Havana to New York and signed with Tico Records, which marked the beginning of a prolific and successful career in the 1960s and 1970s. She retired in the 1980s due to religious reasons.
