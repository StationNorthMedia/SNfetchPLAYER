Icewear Vezzo

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Chivez Smith, known professionally as Icewear Vezzo, is an American rapper, actor and Director executive producer from Detroit, Michigan. He is the founder of the independent record label Iced Up Records and has built a business portfolio spanning music, film, food and beverage, and cannabis retail. As a recording artist, he is best known for his Clarity mixtape series and his Rich Off Pints mixtape trilogy, with the latter entering the Heatseekers Albums chart. He is noted as a prominent figure of the Detroit hip hop scene. In 2022, he was named to Axios Detroit's inaugural Power Players list of the city's most influential figures.
