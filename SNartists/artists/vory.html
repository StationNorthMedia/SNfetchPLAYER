Vory

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Tavoris Javon Hollins Jr., known professionally as Vory, is an American rapper, singer and songwriter. Raised in Louisville, Kentucky, he is best known for his guest appearances on Kanye West's 2021 songs "God Breathed", "Jonah" and "No Child Left Behind" — all of which appear on West's tenth album, Donda. His contributions to the album earned him nominations for Best Rap Album and Album of the Year at the 64th Annual Grammy Awards.
