Shawn Stockman

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Shawn "Slim" Stockman is an American singer, songwriter and record producer. He is best known as a member of the vocal group Boyz II Men. In addition to Boyz II Men, Stockman was a member of the group Black Men United. He was also a judge on the NBC television show The Sing-Off for five seasons (2009–2014).
