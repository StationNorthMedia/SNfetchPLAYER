Benny Spellman

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Benny Spellman was an American R&B singer, he was best known for the 1962 single "Lipstick Traces " and its B-side "Fortune Teller", both written by Allen Toussaint.
