Sparkle

▶️ YouTube 🎵 Deezer 🎧 Spotify 🍎 Apple Music
Stephanie Edwards, known professionally as Sparkle, is an American R&B singer from Chicago, Illinois. She began her career in 1998 as a protégé of producer R. Kelly. Sparkle is best known for her 1998 debut single "Be Careful", which peaked at number 7 on the UK singles chart.
